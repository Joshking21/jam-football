Manager sacked after five straight defeats leave the club ninth
An empty dugout at The Lanternworks last night. Picture: Gazette staff
Port Calloway acted within hours of the 0–3 home defeat to Saltmere, a result that left the title favourites ninth and closer to the drop than the top. Owner Margaret Holloway said the club "could not wait any longer". Theo Vance takes charge until a replacement is found. Supporters' groups had called for change after the derby, the fifth loss in a row.

Dear Alex,

The board met this morning. After five defeats in a row and a fall to ninth, we have decided to end your time as manager, effective today.

You were brought here to win this league. We gave you the budget and the squad to do it, and we have run out of time to wait for the turnaround. Thank you for your work. I wish you well.
