# Dressing room (talk scene) concept (approved by the user, 22 Nov)

Two drawn concepts of the M6b dressing room (§11.25.1, §11.25.5, §14.11.2):
- `talking.png`: a **speaking beat**. Only the caption and a skip hint are on screen.
- `choose.png`: a **choice beat**. The team-talk choice card shows the 6 tones, each with a sample line and the predicted reaction.

The room is a U of lockers and benches, the squad sits facing the middle, the coach stands on the crest, and Theo is by the door. These drawings show layout, staging and UI. The real scene is 3D with Meshy models and painted face layers (ART-DIRECTION).
