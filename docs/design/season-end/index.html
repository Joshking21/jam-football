Mariners finish runners-up, their highest place in 31 years, after a final-day win at The Lanternworks
Lap of honour: the squad salute the North Stand after the final whistle. Picture: Gazette staff
Nobody at The Lanternworks will forget this one. Written off in August as a mid-table side with a new manager and a thin squad, Port Calloway were in the title race until the final fortnight, and finished with a 3–1 win over Ironvale. Bankole's 16 league goals and Calder's 11 assists did the damage, but the story was the defence: only Saltmere conceded fewer. "We did this together," said the manager. "Now we go again."
