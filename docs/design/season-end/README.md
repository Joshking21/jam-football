# Season End reference (approved by the user, 23 Nov)

`season-end.png` is the target for the **last step, "The verdict"**, of the §11.20 sequence. `index.html` is its static source. The earlier steps (final table, awards, in numbers) reuse the League, Week Summary and Full-time components at full size. A step bar on the top right shows Final table · Awards · In numbers · The verdict · Share.

- **Left: the souvenir edition** of the user's local paper (see the Week Summary README, "Local papers").
  - A tall front page tilted −1.4°, with "Souvenir edition" and a "pull-out" line, the masthead, and a dateline under double rules.
  - **Headline:** 100+ px, from a season-outcome template (champions, runners-up, a top-half finish, survival, relegation, sacked).
  - **Standfirst:** with a real fact ("highest place in 31 years" comes from the club's history field; if there's none, the margin to the target).
  - **Press photo:** in halftone. The final-day lap of honour, or a dejected squad for a bad season (rendered from the last match's 3D scene).
  - **Article:** a caption and a three-column article.
- **Right:**
  - **Hero:** the final position (150 px numeral and ordinal), the result line, and a sentence comparing the finish to the board's target. A circular **grade stamp** (A+, A, B, B−, C, D, F per §12.16; red for D and F) that lands last.
  - **The board's verdict:** the owner's printed portrait, a quote picked by grade (`club.json` owner lines), and the board and fans meters. In M6b this becomes the boardroom scene (§11.25.5) before this screen.
  - **Your season in numbers:** record, goals (with a rank note), best win, top scorer, biggest crowd, signings.
  - **Awards row:** the five §12.15 awards; the user's winners are in accent.
  - **"The season, matchday by matchday":** the user's league position as a line over all 18 matchdays against the champion's line, with a dashed transfer-window mark and up to 2 auto annotations ("Top at MD10").
- **Buttons:** Download share card (`D`, the §11.20 1200×630 PNG), Title screen, **New game** (primary). "Next season" only exists if career mode (§24 stretch) is built.
- **Celebration:** confetti in club colours only for a top-3 finish or a grade ≥ A. A bad season has no confetti, a cold grey grade stamp and a rainy photo.

All names, numbers and colours in the image are **sample data**. Always use the game's real data.
