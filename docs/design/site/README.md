# Club site concept (approved by the user, 23 Nov)

`site.png` is the layout for §11.25.1 "The site". The club is **the stadium with the training ground beside it**, not a campus.

- **The main stand** (brick, on the side facing the car park) holds the office, boardroom, press room and the corridor. The dressing room and tunnel are inside it, at pitch level.
- **The training ground** is next to the stadium: two pitches, small floodlights, and a pavilion (changing rooms and kit store).
- **The coach's walk** (gold dashes): along the corridor inside the main stand, out of its side door, past the edge of the car park, onto the training ground. In game this is the 2.5 s walk (§11.25.2); free walking (§24) uses the same route.
- **Around it:** the car park (staff, then fans on matchday), roads, and the harbour town behind the stadium.
- Built in M6b as a few connected zones: the stand interior, the outside path, and the training ground. Nothing outside these is walkable.

This is a drawn layout concept, not the final look (see ART-DIRECTION). Names and numbers are sample data.
